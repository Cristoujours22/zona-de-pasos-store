# Zona de Pasos Store MVP
Build a Colombia-only sneaker storefront and operations panel using Next.js, Supabase, and Cloudinary.
The first release uses WhatsApp and human-verified Nequi payments instead of automated payment or shipping integrations.

## Specs
1. **S1 — Brand and market.** The product must use the confirmed name "Zona de pasos store", operate "solo para colombia", display prices in "pesos colombianos", sell "solo tenis para hombre y mujer (adultos)", and support multiple brands entered by administrators.
2. **S2 — Product catalog.** Administrators must be able to "poner marca, precio, nombre y cargar una foto", manage sizes and quantities, and publish products through a public catalog for adult men and women.
3. **S3 — Images.** Product images must use a free-hosting option that can display them on the storefront. The selected MVP service is Cloudinary, with only the asset identifier and delivery URL persisted by the application.
4. **S4 — Cart and WhatsApp checkout.** The buyer must "pasar por el flujo de seleccion de productos, carrito, etc..." before the application generates a structured order and opens WhatsApp. There is no public customer registration and no automated payment gateway in the MVP.
5. **S5 — Buyer and delivery data.** The checkout must collect "todos los datos pertinentes para un envio" for local or national delivery. Local delivery means the destination city equals the admin-configured dispatch-origin city; every other Colombian city is treated as national delivery, with an administrator override.
6. **S6 — Payment.** Product payment is prepaid through Nequi, while "precio de envío contra entrega y lo asume el cliente". Payment proof is accepted only through WhatsApp and "debe pasar por verificacion humana para evitar estafas". A screenshot must never approve payment automatically.
7. **S7 — Shipping.** The administrator must be able to enter the shipping cost later, select the carrier, add a tracking number, and expose order tracking to the buyer. Carrier API automation is deferred; the MVP uses manual quotes from Colombian carriers such as Envía, Inter Rapidísimo, Coordinadora, or Servientrega.
8. **S8 — Orders.** Administrators must update order states and shipment details. The minimum state flow is pending confirmation, awaiting payment, payment confirmed, preparing, shipped, delivered, and canceled. Payment confirmation must record the acting administrator, timestamp, confirmed amount, and an optional internal note.
9. **S9 — Inventory.** Inventory is deducted manually after a human confirms the sale. Public stock labels must support normal/full stock, low stock, and out of stock with configurable thresholds and visibility; the administrator can hide detailed labels and show only availability. Checkout must warn that availability is subject to confirmation.
10. **S10 — Configurable settings.** Administrators must be able to modify WhatsApp, Nequi details, dispatch-origin city and address, local-delivery classification, carriers, tracking links, inventory thresholds, stock-label visibility, and general store information from the panel.
11. **S11 — Administrative roles.** Store administrators manage catalog, inventory, orders, payments, shipping, and commercial settings. The developer is a superadministrator with maintenance visibility, user/role management, diagnostics, and full environment access. Every person must use an individual account and important actions must be audited.
12. **S12 — Secure onboarding.** There must be no permanent public administrator registration form. A one-time setup form creates the first developer-superadministrator only while no superadministrator exists, requires a deployment secret, permanently disables after success, and is followed by private single-use administrator invitations with expiration and role assignment.
13. **S13 — Customer support.** The public storefront must include a configurable WhatsApp customer-service button.
14. **S14 — Deferred scope.** Logo design, returns policy, automated Nequi verification, carrier API integration, and customer accounts are not part of the initial implementation.
15. **S15 — Visual direction.** The storefront should combine a campaign-led hero, practical product discovery, editorial spacing, mobile-first layouts, visible sizing, clear shipping/payment information, and prominent WhatsApp conversion paths inspired by Nike, Foot Locker, and Kith without copying their protected branding or assets.
